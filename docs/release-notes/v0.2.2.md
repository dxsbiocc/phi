## What's Changed

### Over 1 GB removed from unpacked application footprint

- **Eliminated unused local ONNX and speech runtimes**: excluded optional upstream `@huggingface/transformers`, `onnxruntime-{common,node,web}`, `sherpa-onnx*`, `fastembed`, and `sharp` / `@img` packages from the packaged bundle, with build-time `afterPack` guards preventing regressions. Phi routes all AI requests through configured model APIs without shipping local inference runtimes on user machines.
- **Removed duplicate renderer source packages from `app.asar`**: moved 37 build-time UI, icon, and visualization packages already bundled by `electron-vite` into `devDependencies`, reducing packaged `node_modules` from ~1.34 GB to **323 MB** (> 1 GB smaller unpacked footprint and faster installer downloads across macOS, Linux, and Windows).

## 中文摘要

- **彻底剔除未使用的本地 ONNX 与离线语音推理包**：从安装包与解包目录中完全移除上游可选的 `@huggingface/transformers`、`onnxruntime-{common,node,web}`、`sherpa-onnx*`、`fastembed` 及 `sharp` / `@img` 二进制库，并在 `afterPack` 阶段加入防回退校验；所有模型请求统一走已配置的 API，不再向用户设备塞入本地小模型推理包袱。
- **清理 `app.asar` 中重复打包的纯前端源码库**：将 37 个已在构建期由 `electron-vite` 摇树打包的前端 UI、图标与可视化依赖归入 `devDependencies`，随包 `node_modules` 从约 1.34 GB 直降至 **323 MB**（解压后主程序体积缩减超过 **1 GB**，安装包下载与应用内更新更快）。
